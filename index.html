Shivaya Ki Mummy,

Maine ye page sirf isliye banaya hai taki jab bhi tum kabhi ye socho ki shayad mera pyaar kam hai, tum bas isse padh lena. Kyuki kuch ehsaas lafzon se nahi, sirf dil se samjhe jaa sakte hai.

Pata hai sabse ajeeb baat kya hai har baar mujhe lagta hai ki me tumse kam pyaar karta hu. Aur phir mera dil kehta hai nahi... usse aur pyaar kar, aur uske kareeb aa, aur usse itna chah ki uski har muskurahat me tera naam bas jaaye.

Mujhe na duniya chahiye, na kisi aur khushi ki talaash hai. Bas tera haath meri ungliyon me rahe, aur tera pyaar meri har saans ke saath rahe. Mere liye itna hi kaafi hai.

Shayad kabhi kabhi tujhe lagta hoga ki me itna pyaar nahi karta, ya me apni feelings utni dikhata nahi. Par sach ye hai ki me jitna tumhe mehsoos karta hu, utna lafzon me kabhi bayaan hi nahi kar paata.

Har din uthkar mujhe bas ek hi baat mehsoos hoti hai... Aaj bhi tujhe aur pyaar karna baaki hai. Aaj bhi tujhe aur hasaana baaki hai. Aaj bhi tujhe aur apna banana baaki hai. Kyuki mera dil har roz tumhare liye ek nayi mohabbat lekar uthta hai.

Meri mohabbat ki koi inteha nahi hai. Har subah tera naam meri muskurahat ban jaata hai, aur har raat tera khayal meri neend ban jaata hai. Jitna waqt guzarta hai, utna hi me tumse aur gehra pyaar karne lagta hu.

Agar kabhi tujhe lage ki me tumse kam pyaar karta hu... toh bas meri aankhon me dekh lena. Waha tujhe meri har baat se zyada meri mohabbat nazar aayegi.

Har janam me meri pehli dua, meri aakhri khushi aur meri sabse khoobsurat mohabbat... sirf tum ho Muskan. ❤️
